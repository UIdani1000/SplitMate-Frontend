What is a SplitMate ID?

  • It's a **social identity** (e.g., daniel.mate) for your wallet.
  • Use it to **add friends** to bills instead of pasting long addresses.
  • It maps **one ID to one wallet** address on each chain.
  • Registration is a **one-time $1 fee**, paid in the native token of the chain you register on (ETH, SOL, etc.).
